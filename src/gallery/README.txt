Drop photos in this folder and they appear in the Gallery page automatically.

  - Put images in a sub-folder to make a filter chip (e.g. "Day 1", "Backstage").
  - Images directly in this folder are listed under "Moments".
  - The filename becomes the caption: "05-battle-of-bands.jpg" -> "battle of bands".
    A leading number only controls the order.
  - jpg, jpeg, png, webp, avif, gif are supported.
